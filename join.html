Openings

We are recruiting

  • PhD students with strong fundamentals in ML, vision, or robotics
  • Research interns for 3 to 12 month projects
  • Visiting students and research collaborators

How to Apply

Suggested application materials

  • CV with publications, projects, and technical skills
  • Short statement describing research interests and fit
  • Transcript or portfolio if relevant
  • One to three representative papers or code samples

Who Fits Best

Qualities you can explicitly list

Curiosity

Research taste

Enjoys reading papers, asking good questions, and refining ideas through experiments.

Execution

Strong implementation

Comfortable with coding, debugging, training pipelines, and reproducible evaluation.

Collaboration

Team mindset

Communicates clearly, works well with others, and shares credit generously.

FAQ

Questions to answer upfront

Do applicants need prior publications? No, but evidence of strong research potential is helpful.

Can undergraduates join? Yes, especially for structured project-based internships.

How should applicants contact the lab? Send a short email with CV and a concise fit statement.
