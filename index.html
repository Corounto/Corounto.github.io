Aether Reality

Aether Reality focuses on World Models, Spatio-Temporal Data Mining, and Scientific Machine Learning. We study learning systems that can understand dynamic environments, model complex spatio-temporal processes, and support reliable scientific reasoning.

Research Focus

Learning models for dynamic worlds and scientific systems.

Our work connects perception, forecasting, planning, and physics-grounded learning, with applications in robotics, weather, Earth systems, and scientific simulation.
